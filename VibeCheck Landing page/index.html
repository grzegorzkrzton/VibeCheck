What exactly is VibeCheck?

VibeCheck is an internal Chrome extension for reviewing live, vibe-coded prototypes. It puts comments, wording suggestions, and design inspection tools on the page you’re discussing.

Who is it for?

Product designers, content designers, PMs, and engineers who want to give specific feedback and work through details together.

Does it change my prototype’s code?

Live text edits let you try a change in the browser. They don’t update your source code. AI Prompt helps you copy those text edits into a prompt for your coding tool.

Can we review a prototype together?

Shared feedback keeps comments and wording suggestions attached to a prototype. Sign-in and backend access are required for the shared experience.

How do I get started?

VibeCheck is being prepared for internal testing. Follow your pilot’s approved access process. You can read the installation notes here. This website’s access form is a local demonstration.
